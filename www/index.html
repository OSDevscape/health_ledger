About Health Ledger

Health Ledger

Version 1.0.0 · Offline-first

Your health records stay on this device unless you choose to export them.

This app supports organization and reminders. It does not replace medical advice.

How do reminders work?

Active medications with scheduled times can receive repeating local notifications in the Android app. As-needed medications do not receive automatic reminders. Browser preview mode saves your schedule but does not deliver native notifications.

Why might exact alarm access be requested?

Some Android versions require a separate Alarms & reminders setting for notifications to appear close to the selected time. You can still save your medication schedule if that access is unavailable.

What if reminders do not appear?

Check notification permission, the Pill reminders channel, battery restrictions and Alarms & reminders settings. Then use Resync reminders to refresh schedules.
