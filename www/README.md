# Health Ledger

Your daily health record, organized.

Health Ledger is a simple, offline-first Android app for managing medication routines and tracking glucose readings. It helps you see what’s scheduled, record what you’ve taken, and keep your health information organized in one place.

## What It Does

### Medication tracking

- Keep a personal medication list with dosages, instructions, and notes.
- Set multiple reminder times.
- Choose daily, weekday, custom-day, or as-needed schedules.
- Set start and end dates.
- Pause and resume medications without deleting them.
- Record remaining medication quantities and low-supply thresholds.

### Daily check-ins

- See today’s scheduled medications at a glance.
- Mark doses as taken or skipped, and undo an action when you need to correct it.
- Add a note to any dose, and pick an optional reason when you skip one.
- View daily progress, including scheduled, taken, and remaining doses.
- Review 7-day and 30-day adherence summaries: percent taken, per-medication breakdown, daily strip and skip reasons.

### Glucose tracking

- Record glucose readings in mg/dL or mmol/L.
- Add meal context, dates, times, and notes.
- View your latest reading and previous entries.
- Explore glucose trends over 7 days, 30 days, or all time.
- Edit or delete readings.

### Appointments

- Track visits and checkups with provider, type, format, location and notes.
- Choose reminders 1 day, 2 hours, 1 hour or 15 minutes before.
- Mark appointments completed, missed or canceled, reschedule them, or reopen them.
- Reminders are rescheduled automatically when you edit, reschedule or change status, and removed on delete.

### Reminders and personalization

- Receive scheduled medication reminders with **Mark taken**, **Skip** and **Snooze** buttons. On Android these buttons open Health Ledger briefly to save your choice.
- Choose the snooze length (5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes).
- Use **Reminder health** to check permission, channel, exact-alarm access and scheduled counts, and to send a test reminder.
- Refresh reminder schedules with Resync reminders.
- Choose system, light, dark, or Brown & Gold themes.

### Data and privacy

Your records stay on your device unless you choose to export them.

- Export your health records (medications, doses, glucose, appointments, settings) as a backup. The last backup time is shown in Settings.
- Import a backup with a preview of what it contains compared with this device, then a restore summary. A restore can be undone.
- Try sample data to explore the app.
- Clear locally stored records when needed.

## Important Notes

As-needed medications do not receive automatic reminders. Reminder delivery depends on device settings and permissions.

Keep a backup of important records and do not rely on reminders as your only safeguard.

Health Ledger supports personal organization and recordkeeping. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or replace guidance from a healthcare professional.
